# Physical-device recording for Apple's Guideline 2.1 request

Use the exact submitted build installed through TestFlight on a physical iPhone running the latest publicly available OS compatible with that device. Test the app on supported physical iPhone and iPad devices before resubmitting; record device, OS, version and build accurately. Existing simulator marketing previews do not satisfy this request.

Before recording:
- Use a dedicated disposable test account for registration/deletion. Keep Apple's supplied reviewer account alive.
- Have a second contributor's appropriate, approved recipe available so Report recipe and Block contributor are visible. Do not simulate buttons or fabricate an approval. If no public community recipe exists, submit a harmless, original recipe through the normal flow and have the authorized moderator review it before recording.
- Prepare an email inbox for confirmation and a real product barcode with known database coverage. Hide unrelated private notifications; avoid exposing passwords or email confirmation codes in a publicly shared recording.
- Enable iOS Screen Recording. Start outside the app so the first action shown is tapping INSTEAD to launch. A review demonstration can be longer than the 15–30 second promotional previews.

Record in this order:
1. Launch INSTEAD. Show first-use acknowledgement if presented.
2. Browse, search, filter, open a guide; show ingredients, instructions, storage and cautions.
3. Save a guide or recipe; show Saved guides.
4. Demonstrate optional barcode scanning and the resulting lookup.
5. Register the disposable account, complete email confirmation, and return to the app. Do not omit the completion of registration.
6. Sign out and sign back in. Show My INSTEAD and synced saves.
7. Submit an original recipe with any optional photo you own; show its pending moderation status.
8. Open a published recipe from the second contributor; complete a report and show confirmation. Block that contributor and show the result/blocked-contributor controls. Use a clearly documented test report so the moderator can identify it.
9. Delete the disposable account through My INSTEAD's account controls. Show completion, not just the initial deletion request. If deletion is pending or fails, fix and retest before claiming success.
10. Stop recording. Confirm the demo account Apple will use still works.

There are no paid features to demonstrate in the current app. Add timestamps to the review notes. Attach the recording through App Review Information or provide a link Apple can open without requesting access; verify playback while signed out. Do not publish passwords in a video link.

Finish the notes draft by replacing every bracketed placeholder and removing unverified statements. Confirm regional behavior and content rights. Put credentials directly in Apple's protected review fields. Do not resubmit a draft with placeholders.
